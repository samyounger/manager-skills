# Sources and further reading

These sources support the capacity, forecasting and delivery-confidence practices in this skill. They are decision aids, not a substitute for the organisation's roadmap, work-management data and team judgement.

## Scrum and sprint planning

- [The Scrum Guide (official)](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html) — Sprint Planning considers why the sprint is valuable, what can be done, and how the work will be done; past performance and projected capacity inform the forecast.
- [Sprint planning — Atlassian Agile Coach](https://www.atlassian.com/agile/scrum/sprint-planning) — practical discussion of defining the sprint goal and matching planned work to team capacity.

## Capacity and velocity

- [Capacity planning — Atlassian Team Playbook](https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/capacity-planning) — collaborative capacity planning and making availability explicit.
- [Scrum metrics — Atlassian Agile Coach](https://www.atlassian.com/agile/scrum/scrum-metrics) — capacity/availability, a contingency buffer, velocity and visibility of work-type mix.
- [Sprint velocity — Atlassian](https://www.atlassian.com/agile/project-management/velocity-scrum) — calculate velocity from fully completed stories and use multiple sprints for forecasting.
- [Jira Cloud velocity chart documentation](https://support.atlassian.com/jira-software-cloud/docs/view-and-understand-the-velocity-chart/) — Jira's interpretation of completed estimates and average velocity.

## Forecasting and delivery decisions

- [How Big Things Get Done](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/714549/how-big-things-get-done-by-bent-flyvbjerg-and-dan-gardner/) — reference-class forecasting: use outcomes from comparable work to temper inside-view estimates.
- [“20% for tech debt” doesn’t work](https://newsletter.manager.dev/p/20-for-tech-debt-doesnt-work) — makes maintenance work visible and tied to business value rather than treating it as invisible capacity.
- [Why sprints are taking the joy out of building software](https://newsletter.manager.dev/p/why-sprints-are-taking-the-joy) — sprint anti-patterns and the value of retaining breathing room.
